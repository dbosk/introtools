We distributed the LEQ 6 survey to all participants. They could fill it 
sometime between 2023-09-15 and 2024-06-16.

No changes.

The course is designed to be possible to take asynchronously, in your own pace. 
It's a voluntary course given in parallel to all other courses the students 
take. Most need the terminal and Git early on, but the LaTeX part later. Some 
students complete the course more than a year later, which is fine and it 
works.

Much less than the 1.5 credits they get. But it depends on how you take the 
course.

The course is designed so that there is a lot that *can* be learned. But the 
assessment assesses the basics, which can be done in much less than 40 hours 
(1.5 credits).
