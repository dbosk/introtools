## Any time

Use the
[Discussions](https://canvas.kth.se/courses/64041/discussion_topics "Discussions"){api-endpoint="https://canvas.kth.se/api/v1/courses/64041/discussion_topics"
api-returntype="[Discussion]"}
feature[](https://canvas.kth.se/courses/64041/discussion_topics "General, informal course discussion"){api-endpoint="https://canvas.kth.se/api/v1/courses/64041/discussion_topics"
api-returntype="[Discussion]"}. Post your question and you will
eventually get an answer.

## During lab sessions

To get help during the lab sessions (in period 1, see [your
schedule](https://cloud.timeedit.net/kth/web/) for when they are), you
should do the following.

Set up [Zoom](https://zoom.us/download) and host a new meeting (see
fig. [1](#fig:new)). In the meeting room, select "Participants" at the
bottom of the screen and find the "Invite" button (see
fig. [2](#fig:participants)). Then click "Copy invite link" (see
fig. [3](#fig:invite)).

Head to the [help queue](http://queue.csc.kth.se/Queue/DD1301). Use the
invite link as the location and hit "join queue" to join the queue to
wait for help (see fig. [4](#fig:queue)). When it's your turn, a tutor
will join your Zoom meeting.

## Help after the course and during periods 2\--4 {#help-after-the-course}

After the course (i.e. after the first three weeks of period 1), help
within the course is limited to other students replying in the Canvas
forum. But you can find help from the tutors of
[Allmänhandledningen](https://www.kth.se/social/group/allmanhandledning/).
